# The Problem & Our Solution

## The Global Physiotherapy Crisis

### 1. The Supervision & Compliance Gap
Traditional physical therapy relies heavily on in-clinic visits followed by "homework" exercises given on printed handouts. 
- **The Problem:** Over 70% of patients fail to adhere to their home exercise routines, or they perform the exercises with incorrect form. 
- **The Consequence:** This leads to delayed recovery, re-injury, and chronic pain because there is no supervision when the patient is at home.

### 2. The Accessibility & Cost Barrier
- **The Problem:** Frequent clinical visits are expensive (often ₹500–₹2,000 per session in India) and time-consuming.
- **The Consequence:** Elderly patients, individuals recovering from severe trauma, and those living in rural areas struggle to travel to tier-1 city hospitals, resulting in abandoned rehabilitation plans.

### 3. The Clinical Blind Spot
- **The Problem:** When a patient returns for a 2-week follow-up, the doctor has no objective data on what the patient actually did at home.
- **The Consequence:** Treatment adjustments are made based on subjective patient memory ("I did them mostly right") rather than hard biomechanical data.

---

## The PhysioCare Solution

PhysioCare bridges the gap between the clinic and the living room using edge AI and computer vision directly in the web browser.

### 1. Intelligent Home Supervision (Zero Hardware)
- We turn the patient's existing smartphone, tablet, or laptop webcam into a clinical-grade motion tracker. 
- The system visually measures joint angles in real-time and provides a live HUD with a repetition counter and a Form Accuracy Score, ensuring exercises are done perfectly.

### 2. Multilingual Voice Coaching
- Instead of reading confusing diagrams, the patient receives instant, spoken feedback (e.g., "Bend your knee a little more," "Keep your back straight") in their native language, including Hindi, Odia, Tamil, Telugu, and English.

### 3. Clinical Telemetry & Triage Portal
- Doctors receive a dashboard showing exactly how many reps were completed, the patient's exact range of motion, and their self-reported pain levels.
- Patients are automatically triaged (Red, Yellow, Green) so doctors know immediately who needs a follow-up call, allowing clinics to manage 5x more patients without sacrificing care quality.

### 4. Privacy by Design
- All video processing happens locally on the user's device. No video feeds are ever recorded, stored, or sent to the cloud, ensuring total patient privacy and HIPAA compliance.
